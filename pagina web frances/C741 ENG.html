FAAC 741

Automation for sliding gates weighing up to 1200 Kg


    FAAC 741

    110v Gear Motors

  • Simplified Programming

    • Thanks to the display and the pre-wired rotating board, managing your sliding gate with the FAAC 741 gear motors has never been easier.

  • Magnetic Limit Switch

    • Thanks to the magnetic limit switch, FAAC 741 offers flawless performance even in harsh outdoor environments, ensuring long-lasting operation.

  • Precision and Safety

    • With the built-in encoder of the FAAC 741 gear motors, you can precisely control the opening and braking of your sliding gate.

    If it detects obstacles, the system immediately activates a reverse movement, protecting people and vehicles.

PRODUCT FOCUS:   FAAC 741

Different power, same reliability.

Whether you have a light or heavy gate, FAAC has the solution for you. With the 740 model you can automate sliding gates with leaves of up to 500 kg, while the more powerful FAAC 741 lets you manage gates of up to 1200 kg.




Simplified Setup and Installation

Thanks to the practical LCD display, you can program the automation easily and quickly. The integrated 740D electronic board allows you to connect both traditional and Bus photocells, while the self-learning logic simplifies installation.
