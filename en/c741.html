FAAC 741

Sliding gate automation for gates up to 900 Kg


    FAAC 741

    110v Gearmotors

  • Simplified Programming

    • Thanks to the display and the pre-wired and rotatable card, managing your sliding gate with FAAC 741 gearmotors has never been easier.

  • Magnetic Limit Switch

    • Thanks to the magnetic limit switch, the FAAC 741 offers impeccable performance even in challenging outdoor environments, ensuring long-lasting operation.

  • Precision and Safety

    • With the built-in encoder of the FAAC 741 gearmotors, you can precisely control the gate opening and braking.

    If obstacles are detected, the system immediately reverses the movement, protecting people and vehicles.



    Max leaf weight 900 kg
    Max single leaf width 15 m
    Max speed 14 m/min
    Usage frequency S3 - 40%





PRODUCT FOCUS:   FAAC 741

Different power, same reliability.

Whether you have a light or heavy gate, FAAC has the solution for you. With model 741, you can automate sliding gates with leaves of up to 900 kg.





Simplified configuration and installation

Thanks to the practical LCD display, you can program the automation quickly and easily. The integrated 740D electronic card allows connection to both traditional and Bus photocells, while the self-learning logic simplifies installation.
