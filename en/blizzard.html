PRODUCT FOCUS: Genius Blizzard





Robustness, Reliability, High Performance

The Genius Blizzard 800Kg is also available in 110V version for the Dominican market. With 610 W power consumption, irreversible gearmotor, and electronic limit switches, it manages gates up to 800 kg and offers a maximum speed of approximately 12 m/min. Includes manual key release, compatibility with 120 remote controls, soft stop at end of travel, and anti-collision protection – the robust and safe solution for every home in Santo Domingo.




Practical Installation and Intelligent Management

The Blizzard 800 motor is equipped with an integrated control unit with LCD screen for quick and intuitive programming. Compatible with traditional photocells, it includes auto-learning function for simplified commissioning, even in residential 110V installations.
