Automation for Sliding Gates

FAAC sliding gate automation provides easy and safe access thanks to a smooth and reliable gate movement system.

These solutions are ideal to ensure maximum comfort and security thanks to cutting-edge technologies designed to meet even the most complex requirements.

Our automation systems are designed to be installed on any type of sliding gate and integrate perfectly in any context: from private residences to condominium complexes, as well as commercial and industrial facilities of any size.

To correctly choose a sliding gate automation, it is necessary to consider two fundamental elements: the gate weight and the type of use.






Technology

Hydraulic and Electromechanical

These are the two technologies that distinguish FAAC sliding gate automation today.

Both are designed to meet specific needs and provide maximum flexibility.

Hydraulic

Electromechanical


Hydraulic

Hydraulic technology has always been the symbol of FAAC excellence and remains our strength. We were the first to introduce it, in 1965, for gate automation.

Using hydraulics in this sector requires a very high technological level and absolute precision in assembly.

This allows us to offer all the advantages of this technology, applicable in multiple contexts with consistently excellent performance.

  • Extraordinary durability: very long lifespan, at least 4 times higher than electromechanical equivalents.

  • High force: powerful and compact devices, twice that of electromechanical equivalents.

  • Exceptional resistance: ideal for harsh and aggressive environments (dust, sand, or salt) and high-frequency use.

  • Maximum safety: mechanical force regulation via bypass valves for absolute security.

  • Easy maintenance: easy to maintain and repair, minimal effort required from operators.

  • Smooth and silent movement: almost imperceptible, for a more comfortable experience.

  • Reversibility: reversible operators available in one or both directions, ensuring safe opening from both sides.

Electromechanical

Electromechanical technology stands out for its simplicity. It is developed for those looking for automation with excellent value for money.

The components that transfer power from the motor to the gate are mechanical.

  • Excellent value for money: excellent automation at competitive prices, ideal for those seeking efficiency and convenience.

  • Greater simplicity: essential design ensuring ease of use and maintenance.

  • Proven know-how: a wealth of field experience and competence ensuring maximum reliability.

  • Wide range of products: to meet even specific contextual needs, including integrated products.
