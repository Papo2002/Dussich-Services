FAAC 884

Automation for sliding gates with a weight of up to 3,500 kg.


    FAAC 884 MC 3PH

    Three-phase gear motor

    New integrated E844 3PH electronic board with screen and button programming.

    Double-disc clutch in oil bath.

    Braking device: short stopping distances and secure gate closure.

    Steel casing with cataphoresis treatment and polyester coating.



    Maximum leaf weight 3,500 kg
    Maximum leaf width 42 m
    Maximum speed 10 m/min

Product focus:   FAAC 884 MC 3PH


Industrial power, guaranteed reliability

Robust design for intensive use. Its 850 W three-phase gear motor supports loads of up to 3,500 kg and is designed for continuous operation even in demanding environments.

Smooth and controlled operation. Thanks to the double-disc clutch in an oil bath, it allows fine adjustment of operating force, reducing impacts or vibrations.

Integrated safety. The motor includes automatic locking when not in use, preventing unwanted openings, and thermal protection to extend its service life.

Durable construction. Weather-resistant housing with IP55 protection and anti-corrosion treatment, ideal for harsh outdoor environments.




Precise control in every movement

Its mechanical limit switches (with lever and roller) allow precise definition of opening and closing points. It also includes a manual release system for operation in case of power failure.
